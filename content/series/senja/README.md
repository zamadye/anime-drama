# Senja — Draft Series Bible & Proposed Six-Episode Arc
Status: **draft** (proposed example story data, NOT approved episode canon).

## Core Premise & Tone
- **Language**: Indonesian (`id`).
- **Audience & Format**: Serialized 8–12 minute Modern Series B episodic drama at 1920×1080, 24fps.
- **Premise**: Quiet urban family drama centered on unfinished apologies before departure at an evening railway junction.

## Locked Identities (Source Facts)
1. **Arga (27)** — Architect carrying unresolved regret; restrained posture, navy overcoat, gray shirt, charcoal trousers, dark brown leather boots.
2. **Kirana (25)** — Illustrator leaving town; terracotta cardigan, cream high-neck blouse, plum pleated midi skirt, brown ankle boots, beauty mark under left eye.
3. **Pak Hendra (54)** — Kirana's father, retired train driver; broad shoulders, salt-and-pepper hair, slate blazer.
4. **Sena (16)** — Kirana's younger brother; teal hoodie with mustard trim, relaxed teenager cadence.

## Proposed Six-Episode Arc (Unreviewed Draft)
- **E01**: *Kereta Jam Delapan* — Kirana prepares to board the evening train while Arga delays delivering an old sketchbook on the dusk overpass.
- **E02**: *Jadwal Terakhir* — Pak Hendra confronts Arga at the station platform after discovering a folded blueprint left behind.
- **E03**: *Rumah Tanpa Lampu* — Sena tries to hold the household together while hiding his resentment over the silence between his father and sister.
- **E04**: *Hujan di Stasiun Lama* — Flashback and rain-soaked confrontation clarifying what happened at the studio collapse three years earlier.
- **E05**: *Sketsa yang Tertinggal* — Kirana discovers Arga's annotated corrections inside her margin notes; first honest conversation without guarded deflection.
- **E06**: *Senja Berikutnya* — Departure without melodramatic miracle; clear emotional boundary and a genuine reason for the next letter.
